UNTITLED CITY TRADE GAME - NUMBERS SHEET (Claude, v2 DRAFT)
============================================================

Ruleset version: prototype-001 (not played yet, so the version stays)
Based on: Concept GPT Revision 4 (section 46 list)

THESE NUMBERS ARE EXPECTED TO BE WRONG.
The goal is to have something playable, then fix it after tests.
After this version: NO more theoretical balancing. Play the first game.

Short names used below:
  F = Food, E = Energy, M = Materials, T = Technology, $ = Money
  P = Prestige
  L1 / L2 / L3 = City Level 1 (Settlement) / 2 (City) / 3 (Metropolis)
  "specialty" = the resource the city is strong in
    Agricultural = F, Industrial = M, Energy = E, Technology = T


============================================================
CHANGES FROM v1
============================================================

1. Objective deck: 10 -> 12 cards (4 players x 3 cards).
2. Event order: the current event becomes active at the START of the
   round, before production.
3. Crises are now symmetric: 4 specialty crises (one per resource) +
   1 neutral crisis. No specialty exception for Crisis Prestige.
4. Bids reserve Money. Total active bids <= current Money.
5. Transit Network weaker: only Level 3 upkeep -1, no Prestige.
6. Project timing: A = Rounds 3-6, B = Rounds 7-10.
7. Demand table text corrected (Food Security needs no Materials).
8. Objectives: Rapid Development now Round 7. Monument Builder removed.
   New: Balanced Stock, Patient Investor, Big Deal.
9. New section: WATCH LIST (things to measure, not change yet).


============================================================
1-2. STARTING RESOURCES AND MONEY
============================================================

Every city starts at Level 1 with:

  Specialty resource:        4
  Each other resource:       1
  Money:                     6

Example - Agricultural City:
  F 4, E 1, M 1, T 1, $ 6

Design reason:
After round 1 production, every city has lots of its specialty but only
2 of each other resource. Level 2 needs 3 of each.
-> The natural first move is a TRADE in round 1.


============================================================
3-4. PRODUCTION PER ROUND
============================================================

                    Specialty   Each other resource   Money
  Level 1               3               1               2
  Level 2               4               1               3
  Level 3               5               1               4

Production can be increased by buildings, opportunities, projects and
Research Breakthrough. Bonuses add on top of this table.

Total per round (no bonuses):
  L1: 6 resources + 2 $
  L2: 7 resources + 3 $
  L3: 8 resources + 4 $


============================================================
5. STORAGE
============================================================

  F, E, M, T:   10 each
  Money:        unlimited
  Warehouse building: +4 to each of F, E, M, T (-> 14)

Storage is checked only at Round Resolution. During the window, a player
may hold more. Anything above the limit at Round Resolution is discarded.


============================================================
6-7. CITY LEVEL COSTS
============================================================

  Level 2 (City):        3F + 3E + 3M + 3T
  Level 3 (Metropolis):  4F + 4E + 4M + 4T + 4$

- Upgrade is done during the Development & Trade Window.
- Only one level per round.
- New production values start next round.
- Prestige is given at Round Resolution.


============================================================
8. UPKEEP
============================================================

Paid in the Automatic Update step. Money cannot be used.
The city's own specialty cannot be used.

  Level 1:  no upkeep
  Level 2:  1 non-specialty resource
  Level 3:  2 DIFFERENT non-specialty resources

Transit Network building: Level 3 upkeep -1 (2 -> 1).
It has no effect at Level 2.


============================================================
9. STRAINED CITY
============================================================

If upkeep or a crisis payment is not paid in full:
- the city is STRAINED
- NEXT round: specialty production -2 and Money income -1
- the penalty does not stack (see Watch List)
- the city does NOT lose a level
- for upkeep: whatever part the player could pay is still paid
- for crises: no partial payment (pay in full or nothing)


============================================================
10. BUILDINGS
============================================================

Each player may build each building ONCE.
Effects start next round (except Prestige, given at Round Resolution).

  #  Name                Level  Cost                    Effect                          P
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1  Warehouse           L1     1E 1M 1T                Storage +4 (F, E, M, T)         0
  2  Workshop            L1     1F 1M 1T                +1 production of ONE            0
                                                        non-specialty resource
                                                        (chosen when built)
  3  Market Hall         L1     1F 1E 1T + 2$           +1 Money per round              0
  4  Specialty Complex   L2     1F 1E 2M 2T             +1 specialty production        +1
  5  Research Lab        L2     2F 2E 1M                +1 Technology production       +1
  6  Transit Network     L2     2E 2M 1T                Level 3 upkeep -1               0
  7  Civic Center        L2     2F 2E 2M 2T             (no effect)                    +2
  8  Grand Landmark      L3     3F 3E 3M 3T + 5$        (no effect)                    +3

Idea behind the list:
- L1 buildings = economy (no Prestige)
- L2 buildings = economy + a little Prestige
- Transit Network = preparation for Level 3, a choice not an auto-build
- Civic Center and Grand Landmark = pure Prestige, very expensive


============================================================
11-12. GLOBAL MARKET
============================================================

Every resource (F, E, M, T) uses the same price table.

  "Market SELLS" = what the player PAYS to buy 1 resource
  "Market BUYS"  = what the player GETS for selling 1 resource

  Price step   Market SELLS   Market BUYS
  ------------------------------------------
      A             3              1
      B (start)     4              1
      C             5              2
      D             6              3

All four resources start at step B (buy for 4$, sell for 1$).
Human trades around 2-3$ are better for both sides.

MOVEMENT (at Round Resolution, for each resource separately):
  net = units bought FROM the market - units sold TO the market
  net >= +3  -> price moves one step UP   (max D)
  net <= -3  -> price moves one step DOWN (min A)
  otherwise  -> no change

Event modifiers apply only during the event round, on top of the current
step (and stay within A-D).

Market limits:
- Max 4 units of each resource bought per player per round.
- No limit on selling.


============================================================
13. CONTRACT BREAK PENALTIES
============================================================

Formal contract rules (from concept):
- Max duration 3 rounds.
- No obligation may be due after Round 14.
- Mutual cancellation before the due round: free.

When a contract is broken (voluntary OR unable to pay):
- Breaker pays the VICTIM: 2$ for each unpaid resource unit
  (for a Money obligation: the unpaid Money x 2).
- If the breaker cannot pay it all: pays what they have, then loses
  1P for every 2$ still unpaid (round up). The victim does NOT get
  this Prestige.
- Breaker also loses 1P.
- Public "Contracts Broken" counter +1.

Example:
B promised 4 Food in Round 7, has only 1 Food and 3$.
- 1 Food is paid, 3 Food unpaid -> compensation 6$
- B pays 3$, 3$ unpaid -> -2P (3/2 rounded up)
- plus -1P for breaking
- Total: victim gets 1 Food + 3$, B loses 3P, B's counter +1
(Severity is on the Watch List.)


============================================================
14. EVENT CARDS (12)
============================================================

Schedule:
- Round 1: no event.
- Rounds 2-13: one event per round (all 12 cards, shuffled).
- Round 14: no event.
- Warning: the next event is shown one round before.
  The first warning (for Round 2) is shown during setup.

ACTIVATION:
The event becomes active at the very START of its round (step 1.1),
before production. Production and income modifiers therefore apply in
the same round.

CRISIS EVENTS:
- Every L2+ city must pay the printed cost in the World Update step,
  or become Strained. L1 cities are not affected.
- Pay in full -> +1P (Crisis Prestige). No specialty exception.
- Each city has exactly ONE "easy" crisis (its own specialty), and one
  neutral crisis is equally hard for everyone.
- Players prepare in the round before (that is what the warning is for).

  #   Event                   Effect during the event round
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1   DROUGHT                 CRISIS. Every L2+ city pays 2F, or becomes
                              Strained. Food market price +1 step.

  2   ENERGY SHORTAGE         CRISIS. Every L2+ city pays 2E, or becomes
                              Strained. Energy market price +1 step.

  3   SUPPLY SHOCK            CRISIS. Every L2+ city pays 2M, or becomes
                              Strained. Materials market price +1 step.

  4   EPIDEMIC                CRISIS. Every L2+ city pays 2T, or becomes
                              Strained. Technology market price +1 step.

  5   INFRASTRUCTURE FAILURE  CRISIS (neutral). Every L2+ city pays 2
                              DIFFERENT non-specialty resources, or becomes
                              Strained.

  6   CONSTRUCTION BOOM       Every building costs 1 fewer M this round
                              (min 0). Materials market price -1 step.

  7   TECHNOLOGY BOOM         City upgrades cost 2 fewer T this round.

  8   RECESSION               No Money income this round.

  9   TRADE DISRUPTION        Market: sell prices +2$, buy prices -1$
                              (min 1$) this round.

  10  PUBLIC FESTIVAL         Optional, once per player: pay 2F + 2$
                              during the window -> +1P.

  11  RESEARCH BREAKTHROUGH   Optional, once per player: pay 3T during the
                              window -> +1 permanent production of any ONE
                              non-specialty resource.

  12  GOOD HARVEST YEAR       Every city +1 Food and +1 Energy production
                              this round.

Summary: 5 crises, 4 economy events, 3 opportunity events.


============================================================
15. HIDDEN OBJECTIVE CARDS (12)
============================================================

Deal 3, keep 2. Each completed objective = +2P (revealed at the end).
The Banker tracks completion secretly.
Objectives should force UNUSUAL behavior, not reward the normal path.
None of these match a single city specialty.

  #   Name                   Condition
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1   ACTIVE TRADER          Complete at least one instant trade with each
                             of the 3 opponents.
  2   DIVERSIFIED ECONOMY    Own at least 4 different buildings.
  3   PROJECT PARTNER        Qualify as a contributor in both public
                             projects.
  4   CONTRACT PLAYER        Complete 2 formal contracts and never break
                             one.
  5   MARKET INDEPENDENCE    Buy from the global market in no more than
                             3 rounds of the whole game.
  6   RAPID DEVELOPMENT      Reach Level 3 by the end of Round 7.
  7   STEADY CITY            Never become Strained.
  8   OPPORTUNITY WINNER     Win at least one regional opportunity bid.
  9   CRISIS RESPONDER       Earn Crisis Prestige at least 3 times.
  10  BALANCED STOCK         After any Round Resolution, hold at least
                             5 of EACH of F, E, M, T.
  11  PATIENT INVESTOR       Contribute to public projects in at least
                             3 different rounds.
  12  BIG DEAL               Complete one instant trade in which you give
                             at least 5 resources (not Money).

Changes: Crisis Responder raised from 2 to 3 because there are now 5
crises. Monument Builder removed (it rewarded the normal winning path).


============================================================
16. PUBLIC PROJECT CARDS (3, use 2 per match)
============================================================

Shuffle, draw 2.
  Project A: open Rounds 3-6  (deadline = Round Resolution of Round 6)
  Project B: open Rounds 7-10 (deadline = Round Resolution of Round 10)

Success reward starts the round after the deadline:
  Project A reward works for Rounds 7-14 (8 rounds)
  Project B reward works for Rounds 11-14 (4 rounds)
For Project B, the Prestige competition is the main reason to join.

CONTRIBUTION POINTS:
  1 resource = 2 points
  1 Money    = 1 point
Only the resource types printed on the card can be given, and not more
than the card still needs.

Minimum qualifying contribution: 6 points.

  Name                        Needs                 Success reward
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  REGIONAL POWER GRID         6E + 6M + 6$          Qualifying contributors:
                                                    +1 Energy production
  REGIONAL RESEARCH           6T + 4M + 6$          Qualifying contributors:
  INITIATIVE                                        +1 Technology production
  FOOD SECURITY PROGRAM       6F + 4E + 6$          Qualifying contributors:
                                                    +1 Food production

Prestige (only if the project succeeds):
  Largest contributor (points):        +3P
  Tie for largest:                     +2P each
  Other qualifying contributors:       +1P
If the project fails: all contributions are lost, nobody gets anything.


============================================================
17. REGIONAL OPPORTUNITY CARDS (5)
============================================================

Shuffle. One new card appears in Rounds 3, 5, 7, 9, 11.
Secret Money bids in that round's window, resolved at Round Resolution.
Several cards can be open at the same time; each card has its own bid.

MONEY RESERVATION:
- A bid RESERVES that Money until Round Resolution.
- Reserved Money cannot be spent on anything else.
- The TOTAL of a player's active bids cannot be more than their current
  unreserved Money.
- A player may change or withdraw a bid until the window ends.
- At Round Resolution: winners pay; losing and tied bids release the
  reserved Money.

TIES:
Highest bid tied -> nobody wins, nobody pays, the card stays for the next
round and players bid again.

  Name               Benefit (permanent, starts next round)
  -----------------------------------------------------------
  SOLAR FIELD        +1 Energy production
  FERTILE VALLEY     +1 Food production
  INDUSTRIAL ZONE    +1 Materials production
  RESEARCH CAMPUS    +1 Technology production
  COMMERCIAL HUB     +2 Money per round

No Prestige for winning a card. If a card is never won, it is removed
after Round 14.

============================================================
20. TIE AND EDGE RULES
============================================================

BIDS
- 0 = pass. Minimum 1$ to win. Highest bid wins, only the winner pays.
- Tied highest bid: nobody wins, nobody pays, card stays next round.
- Active bids reserve Money; total bids <= unreserved Money.

CONTRACTS
- Cannot pay when due -> automatically broken (penalties in section 13).
- No obligation may be due after Round 14.
- A contract made in Round 14 cannot create a future obligation.

STORAGE
- Checked only at Round Resolution. Excess is discarded.
- Players should sell or spend extra before the window ends.

PROJECTS
- Tie for largest contributor: +2P each.
- A project cannot receive more than it still needs.
- Failure: contributions lost.

LEVELS / BUILDINGS
- One level-up per player per round.
- Any number of buildings per round (if affordable).

CRISES
- Only L2+ cities pay.
- No partial payment: pay in full or become Strained.

EVENTS
- The current event is active from the start of its round.

FINAL TIEBREAKERS (from concept)
1. Higher city level
2. More completed hidden objectives
3. Fewer broken contracts
4. Shared victory


============================================================
21. SETUP STEPS
============================================================

1. Assign cities randomly (one each: Agricultural, Industrial, Energy,
   Technology).
2. Give starting resources: specialty 4, others 1, Money 6.
3. Shuffle the 12 objective cards. Deal 3 to each player; each keeps 2
   and gives the third face down to the Banker. All 12 cards are used.
4. Shuffle the 12 event cards. Reveal the top card as the warning for
   Round 2.
5. Shuffle the 3 project cards, draw 2 (Project A, Project B). Put the
   third away unseen.
6. Shuffle the 5 opportunity cards into a face-down pile.
7. Set all 4 market resources to price step B (sell 4$ / buy 1$).
8. Prepare the round tracker (below) and the Banker sheet.

============================================================
ROUND ORDER (exact)
============================================================

1. AUTOMATIC UPDATE
   1.1 This round's event card becomes ACTIVE (production and income
       modifiers now apply).
   1.2 Apply Strained penalty from last round (then clear Strained).
   1.3 Production (resources + Money).
   1.4 Upkeep. Not paid in full -> Strained (penalty next round).
   1.5 Contract obligations due this round are paid or broken.

2. WORLD UPDATE
   2.1 Crisis payments (if a crisis event). Not paid -> Strained.
   2.2 Show warning for next round's event.
   2.3 New project / opportunity card appears (per tracker).

3. DEVELOPMENT & TRADE WINDOW
   Everything simultaneous: trade, market, build, upgrade, contribute,
   bid, sign contracts, optional event actions (Festival, Breakthrough).

4. ROUND RESOLUTION
   4.1 Open trade offers expire.
   4.2 Secret bids resolve (winners pay, other reservations released).
   4.3 Project deadline resolves (if any).
   4.4 Prestige for this round is added (levels, buildings, projects,
       crises, festival).
   4.5 Storage limits: excess discarded.
   4.6 Market prices move for next round.
   4.7 Temporary event effects end.
   4.8 After Round 14: reveal hidden objectives, final score, winner.

(This is also the fixed step order for the engine's ResolveRound.)

============================================================
WATCH LIST (measure in tests, do NOT change yet)
============================================================

1. LATE-GAME TRADING
   Every city produces +1 of each non-specialty resource every round.
   Trading may drop after the early game.
   Measure: number of player trades per round (early / mid / late).
   Knob if needed: 0 production for one non-specialty resource per city.

2. CONTRACT PENALTY SEVERITY
   One failed obligation can cost about -3P.
   Measure: how many formal contracts are signed at all.
   If almost none: lower the compensation to 1$ per unit.

3. STRAINED DOES NOT STACK
   A player who already failed upkeep can skip a crisis in the same
   round with no extra production penalty.
   Measure: does anyone do this on purpose?
   Prepared fix: -1P for each extra mandatory-payment failure in the
   same round.

4. TRANSIT NETWORK
   Is it now a real choice, or does nobody build it?

5. PROJECT B
   Do players join it only for Prestige? Does it fail more often?

6. OBJECTIVE DIFFICULTY
   Which objectives are completed almost always / almost never?

7. WINNING SCORE
   Is the winner around 14-19P? Is the hidden objective swing too big?


============================================================
TUNING KNOBS (what to change first after tests)
============================================================

If cities level up too fast:      raise Level 2 to 4 each, or starting
                                  "other" resources to 0.
If nobody trades:                 lower non-specialty production to
                                  0 for one resource per city.
If the market is used too much:   raise start price step to C.
If Money has no use:              lower Money income by 1 per level.
If Money is too tight:            start Money 8.
If scores are too low (<12):      lower Civic Center / Landmark cost.
If one crisis hurts too much:     crisis cost 2 -> 1.
If projects always fail:          lower "Needs" by 2 per resource.
If projects always succeed early: raise "Needs" by 2 per resource.
If upkeep is frustrating:         Level 3 upkeep -> 1 resource.

Change only 2-3 knobs per test. Otherwise you cannot know what helped.
Every change after a played game = new ruleset version (prototype-002, ...).
